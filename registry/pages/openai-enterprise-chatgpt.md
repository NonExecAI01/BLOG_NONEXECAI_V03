# ChatGPT Enterprise — Board Governance Reference

**Last verified:** 2026-07-03 | **Type:** enterprise-agent | **Provider:** OpenAI

## Governance summary

ChatGPT Enterprise is OpenAI's business tier for organization-wide AI assistant deployment. Boards should focus on **approved use cases**, admin governance of custom GPTs, and preventing shadow consumer accounts in regulated workflows.

## Board oversight questions

- Which business units are approved for ChatGPT Enterprise vs. consumer accounts?
- Does our AI governance policy cover custom GPTs and plugin integrations?
- What is the reporting cadence on usage anomalies to the audit committee?

## Enterprise controls

SSO, admin console, domain verification, usage analytics, enterprise data not used for training by default.
