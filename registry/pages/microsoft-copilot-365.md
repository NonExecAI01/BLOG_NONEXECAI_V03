# Microsoft 365 Copilot — Board Governance Reference

**Last verified:** 2026-07-03 | **Type:** orchestration-platform | **Provider:** Microsoft

## Governance summary

Microsoft 365 Copilot embeds generative AI across the productivity suite. Board oversight spans **data scope**, Purview controls, identity hygiene, and deployer obligations under the EU AI Act when AI affects workers or compliance-sensitive workflows.

## Board oversight questions

- Has management documented which M365 data scopes Copilot may access?
- Are Purview policies aligned with records retention and legal hold requirements?
- What KPIs demonstrate responsible rollout vs. shadow AI adoption?

## Enterprise controls

Microsoft Entra ID, Purview DLP, audit logs, Copilot controls in Microsoft 365 admin center, data residency options.
